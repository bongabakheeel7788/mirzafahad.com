Insurance in Pakistan suffers a trust deficit with a factual basis: claims are repudiated aggressively, and most refused policyholders simply give up — which is precisely what the repudiation strategy prices in. But the escalation machinery above the insurer is real, free at its most useful level, and statistically friendly to persistent claimants with documents.

## Why claims get refused

Four grounds cover most repudiations. **Material non-disclosure**: the proposal form omitted something — typically health history in life policies — that the insurer now says would have changed underwriting; the battleground is whether the fact was truly material, actually known, and whether the form was filled by the policyholder or by an agent in a hurry (a recurring reality the forums recognise). **Exclusions**: the loss falls, says the insurer, within policy carve-outs — with disputes over interpretation, and the settled principle that ambiguity in the insurer's own document reads against the insurer. **Breach of condition**: late intimation, missing documents, an unapproved workshop — technicalities that succeed only insofar as they genuinely prejudiced the insurer. **Suspicion of fraud**: staged theft, inflated bills — where the insurer carries a heavy burden it often cannot discharge.

## Fight the file first

Repudiations come as letters citing clause numbers. Answer with paper: demand the **complete policy** (many policyholders have only the schedule, never the booklet of terms), your **proposal form** as actually submitted, the **surveyor's report** in property and motor claims — the document repudiations lean on and claimants rarely read — and put your rebuttal in writing against each cited clause. This internal round matters because everything after it reviews this record.

## The Ombudsman: the forum built for you

The **Federal Insurance Ombudsman** exists for exactly this dispute: policyholder complaints against insurers, filed on plain paper with your documents, **without fees and without needing counsel**, decided after hearing both sides in months rather than years, with orders binding on the insurer subject to the framework's review routes. The Ombudsman's jurisprudence is notably unimpressed by technical repudiations — late-intimation refusals without prejudice, agent-filled-form non-disclosure defences, surveyor reports that assumed their conclusions. File within the limitation window after the insurer's final answer, attach the correspondence trail, and quantify precisely what the policy owes.

Above and beside: **SECP**, the sector regulator, receives conduct complaints that discipline repeat behaviour; the **consumer courts** have entertained insurance-service deficiencies; and the **civil courts** remain open, realistic mainly for large commercial claims that justify their pace.

## Life, motor, health: the specific frictions

**Life claims** turn on disclosure fights the family must litigate without the policyholder — which is why policies should be bought with forms personally and truthfully completed, and beneficiaries told where documents live. **Motor claims** turn on surveyor valuations and workshop games; document the vehicle's condition, contest lowball surveys with independent estimates, and remember total-loss arithmetic is negotiable evidence, not decree. **Health claims** turn on pre-authorisation and exclusion lists; fight rejections with the treating doctor's certification of necessity, the instrument the forums credit most.

The pattern across all three: the insurer's advantage is your exhaustion, and the Ombudsman route was engineered to remove exactly that advantage. Persistence, in this arena, is not stubbornness — it is strategy with a strong track record.
